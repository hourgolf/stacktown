# Direction B — the wooden city (reference board)

**Governance (owner-ruled, 2026-08-31): this board is DIRECTION B's
comparison set and is NEVER CITABLE FOR FLAGSHIP WORK.** Mirror
discipline to CANON.md, kept as separate as the two catalogues. The
flagship's canon (8 slots, 5 filled) is untouched by anything here.

**THE DIRECTION, locked by the owner at intake: WOOD IS THE DIRECTION.**
Direction B is an actual wooden planning model — carved timber massing
on an inlaid board — judged with the diorama doctrine's own standards:
details, lighting and camera work all still apply. Where the wood
buys rendering simplicity, the budget goes to creativity and art, not
to shortcuts. Buildings are windowless masses BY DAY; windows exist
only as light at night (B4). Small drawn details survive as discoveries
for the player — never flagship-granular, never absent.

Intake 2026-08-31: four references, quizzed one at a time, owner's
answers recorded per slot. Images live beside this file in
`Docs/direction_b/`.

## B1 — the carved downtown (1739485789466.webp) · THE MATERIAL LOCK

Carved-timber city model: solid wood blocks, massing and roof-form
only, grain as the surface, tones pale pine to dark walnut, roads
etched flat.
- **Wood IS the direction** (owner, definitive), and this image is the
  DETAIL-LEVEL ANCHOR for the whole catalogue.
- Tone variation = **warmth and tonal dynamics** for now; the door to
  tone-as-gameplay is explicitly open for later discussion.
- The boom is **limited considerably but still explores** the model.
- Road/street micro-details: drawn, not granular — "surprise the
  player with the little details they find without going overboard."

## B3 — the living city model (img_5009.jpg) · THE BOARD & TIME

A maintained whole-city planning model: green-stained wood-inlay
river, carved freeway ribbons, terrain, and visibly fresher blocks
where the model was updated over years.
- **PATINA MECHANIC: LOCKED.** New/upgraded buildings start pale and
  age toward the board's honey tone over game time — time made visible
  in the model's native language.
- **Water-laying and terraforming/carving are BUILD VERBS** — the
  player lays water and carves terrain. The board reads as MANY FITTED
  INLAY PIECES making the landscape; that visible pieced construction
  is part of the look.
- Portland resemblance: a HAPPY ACCIDENT — direction B is not tied to
  Portland.
- **This is the SCALE AMBITION** (the board the player eventually
  fills). Long-horizon note, owner verbatim: "eventually this could
  scale to a multiplayer world where each player's model is part of a
  larger network model country/world."

## B4 — the model at night (ziba_04.jpg) · NIGHT & LIGHT

A presentation model in the dark, chosen buildings glowing from inside
through etched window bands, context sleeping grey.
- **Windows are a NIGHTTIME phenomenon**: plain carved mass at noon, a
  lantern at midnight — mood and detail, confirmed read.
- **The glow carries information: ownership AND activity AND
  selection**, conveyed with SUBTLE tone and colour temperature —
  owner's spec: slight-to-bright GREENISH for positive (e.g. office
  performance), soft-to-bright RED for negative, other hues for
  availability / opportunity / alerts. **"The key is subtlety."**
- **Day/night: a toggle while building; an in-game CYCLE at ship** —
  daylight during active market hours, darker when the market closes.
- The unlit grey context = undeveloped board; **progress is literally
  the city lighting up over time.**
