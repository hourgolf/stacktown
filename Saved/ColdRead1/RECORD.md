## What it changes

1. The 0.4% framework is CONFIRMED end to end: distance is carried by
   massing/silhouette (the catalogue bought this) and the close read is
   carried by surface fabrication (polish has not touched this yet).
2. AN OPEN QUESTION, deliberately NOT enshrined as doctrine (owner
   correction, same day): the uniform 'paper' texture was NOT a neutral
   observation - it was one of the things that told the reader
   "rendering". WHY it reads as a tell is not yet isolated. Candidate
   hypotheses, to be settled by measurement (the material-study
   instrument exists for exactly this), not by declaration:
     (a) one fabrication FAMILY everywhere - vehicles would be diecast on
         a real diorama, a different family from card buildings;
     (b) an identical grain SIGNATURE on every surface - the same
         triplanar paper at the same scale and alignment on every object
         is a shader's fingerprint; real card varies sheet to sheet;
     (c) grain SCALE at player zoom - the close read may make the paper
         read as wallpaper rather than material.
   "A model is unified by fabrication" REMAINS the doctrine until a study
   says otherwise. One cold read is a finding, not an amendment.
3. Wave 1 priorities arrive pre-triaged by the reader: close-range
   planar/clipping tells (generator fixes + a coplanarity-class gate
   rule); the PAPER-TELL STUDY above - hypotheses (a)/(b)/(c) one
   variable apart on the study wall, before any material-system change
   ships; the lens rig confirmed load-bearing.
