# The test city — plan of record (owner decisions, 2026-08-30)

The district placer's first real outing, the core gameplay buildings'
debut, and the lighting front's fix — one build, three owner decisions:

1. **SCALE: 4–6 blocks around one arterial crossing.** Two arterials,
   districts legible, variety rules stressed, ~60–100 buildings from
   the 548-mesh catalogue, one honest board rig. Its own NEW MAP,
   created at the GUI by the owner (remote level creation is the
   documented crash trap).
2. **CORE BUILDINGS: BUILD NOW, LOOK-FIRST** (owner's explicit call,
   against the declare-and-wait recommendation — they want to see
   them). Strategy MARKETPLACE and REAL ESTATE OFFICE. Doctrine still
   applies in order: archetype DECLARATION first (look-soul + registry
   entry, function marked TBD-pending-economy-notes), CANON REFERENCE
   before geometry (slots 6–8 are open; the marketplace may absorb the
   wanted "stores reference" slot — owner supplies or blesses images),
   then recipes through genbuild, gated and stamped like everything.
   Function retrofits when the economy notes land; the owner accepts
   that rework risk knowingly.
3. **LIGHTING: re-scoped 2026-08-31 — the investigation runs ON THE
   BENCH, not the test city** (coordinator amendment after the corner-
   order review; the original binding to "the test-city rig" was the
   plan's error). The render-like tell was observed in read #2's
   frames, shot on the bench street: a STABLE, read-validated subject
   with direct comparability to the exact frames the reader judged.
   Chasing it on a city whose massing is actively changing (corners,
   deeper footprints) risks attributing massing to lighting — the
   tree-card between-subject lesson. Corrected rig values then flow
   into citylight.py BY DERIVATION. Runs PARALLEL to the headless
   corner work, in editor windows. Hypothesis grid in
   Saved/ColdRead2/RECORD.md; the owner's eyes decide it.
