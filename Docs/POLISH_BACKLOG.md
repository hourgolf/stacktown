# Polish backlog

Known, reproduced, and deliberately deferred. Everything here was seen in a
rendered image and is described by what is wrong rather than by a guess at the
cause — a backlog entry that names a cause it has not isolated becomes a false
finding, which this project has already paid for once (see `DONORS.md`).

| ~~P9~~ | ~~`cores.py`~~ | **DONE, but the item was wrong.** These three do not need `open_roof` and never did: `build_house`, `build_walkup` and `build_works` each build one solid `Wall_Body` with four real walls — they are buildings, not facade shells, which is why `step_elevations` already excluded them from flanks. | P11 | **Two separate faults, one of them mine, plus a measurement lesson.** <br><br>**(a) Z-FIGHTING — the thing the owner was actually seeing.** Capping the core at `ztop` so roofs would be visible put its top face exactly level with every flat roof's deck top, parapet base and top-floor wall boxes. Three coplanar surfaces per building; the depth buffer had nothing to choose between them, and jagged white patches crawled across every roof. **This is a geometry fault and no amount of GI tuning can fix one** - which is why weeks of lighting work never touched it. Fixed by `cores.ROOF_CLEAR = 17.5`; the half unit is deliberate, since all hand-authored geometry here uses whole numbers and any whole-number clearance can collide by arithmetic accident (14.0 landed on `Band_StackCap`). Verified: 0 coplanar faces across the catalogue, and now gated by **GATE-10**. <br><br>**(b) A LUMEN DRIFT, and I had been making it worse.** Four Lumen cvars were left at 0 from my own diagnostic sweep and never restored - console cvars are session state and appear in no asset and no diff. Restoring them took empty-view drift 1.143% -> 0.222%. `lumen_defaults.py` exists for this now. <br><br>**(c) MY MEASUREMENTS WERE WRONG THREE WAYS.** I measured frame MEAN, which cannot see shimmer at all (half the pixels rise while half fall) - `shimmer.py` measures per-pixel delta instead.
