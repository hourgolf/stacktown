# Archetype definition-of-good worksheet

Prepared 2026-08-28 by the coordinator for the owner + design session.
The archetype gate is live and its contract is MANDATORY: an archetype
declares what "good" means BEFORE any geometry exists
(`Docs/ARCHETYPE_GATE.md`). This worksheet turns that conversation into
an hour of decisions. Fill each page's OWNER CALLS; everything else is
prefilled from the registry, the gate, and the canon, and is correctable.

The gate rules, for reference: 01 roles · 02 materials · 03 detail
density · 04 material count · 05 parcel fit · 06 name collision ·
07 depth behind front third · 08 rear-face parts · 09 glazing per
habitable floor · 10 roof clearance · 11 coplanarity (PENDING, debt
curve). Registry today: `street` (all rules), `industrial` (07/08 exempt,
03 at a PROVISIONAL threshold), `agricultural-structure` (03/07/08/09
exempt) — the latter two were speculative placeholders and these pages
supersede them on approval.

---

## 1. Stores (street-retail)

**Likely verdict: NOT a new archetype.** A store is the vernacular/
contemporary street building's ground floor — the existing recipes carry
shopfronts, canopies, and the tier ladder already tells retail stories
(lock-up → shop & flat). New recipes, maybe; new definition of good, no.
- **Canon coverage:** slots 2 and 5 suffice.
- **OWNER CALLS:** □ confirm stores stay `street` archetype
  □ any retail-specific recipe wanted (market hall? arcade?) — that is a
  recipe request, not an archetype.

## 2. Malls (large-footprint retail)

**The first real test of the depth rules.** A mall is a deep box with an
articulated front and a service rear — GATE-07 (depth) it passes easily,
but GATE-08 (dressed rear) is WRONG for it: a mall's rear is loading
docks, honestly blank walls, and rooftop plant. Closer to `industrial`'s
rear than to `street`'s.
- **Candidate good:** "an articulated public face, a working service
  rear, and a roof that reads as plant-scape at the block hero."
- **Gate sketch:** judges 01-06, 09 (public floors), 10, 11; 07 judged
  (it has depth); 08 REPLACED by a works-yard-style rear rule (docks and
  plant, not windows) — new rule needed, with self-test.
- **Canon coverage:** slot 4 partially (rooftop kit); the public-face
  look is UNCOVERED → slot 6-8 candidate reference needed.
- **OWNER CALLS:** □ is the mall's roof a fifth facade (player sees it
  constantly at zoom-out)? □ era: contemporary only, or a deco "grand
  arcade" variant? □ approve the rear-rule concept.

## 3. Apartments (residential mid/high-rise)

**Extends cleanly — the walkup's grown-up sibling.** A street elevation
with floors, openings, parapet, crown: the generator's home ground. The
question is not the gate (it is `street`, all rules judge) but the
LADDER: your 5+-tier directive (CANON slot 5) lands here first — this is
the recipe family that should reach true tower height.
- **Candidate good:** the street definition unchanged, plus "balconies
  and roof life read at player zoom; the crown varies across neighbours"
  (the comb lesson).
- **Canon coverage:** slot 5 (highrise read); close-up domestic surface
  UNCOVERED.
- **OWNER CALLS:** □ confirm `street` archetype □ tier count for the
  tower ladder (7? 9?) □ does residential get its own palette band
  (walkup pastels) or share commercial's?

## 4. Industrial (warehouses, sheds, depots)

**Registry entry exists; the works blocks are prior art.** Defined by NOT
being an articulated street elevation — long spans, working walls, doors
sized for vehicles, rooflines that are silhouettes (sawtooth, stacks,
tanks) rather than crowns.
- **Candidate good:** "silhouette and rooftop kit carry the read;
  openings follow the work, not the storey; the yard is part of the
  building" (yard rules exist: zonelayout.yard_layout, DONORS vetting).
- **Gate sketch:** as registered (07/08 exempt with printed reasons) —
  BUT the 03 threshold is PROVISIONAL and must be MEASURED from the works
  blocks the city already has before the first catalogue shed is judged.
- **Canon coverage:** slot 4, blessed for exactly this massing and yard
  vocabulary — the one archetype fully covered.
- **OWNER CALLS:** □ ratify the exemptions □ commission the GATE-03
  industrial threshold measurement (works blocks E/H as the sample)
  □ does industrial join the economy's tier ladders (shed → works →
  foundry → plant exists in RECIPES_DRAFT) as-is?

## 5. Agricultural

**The standing recommendation is: mostly NOT BUILDINGS.** Fields are
ground treatment (the scaling review's own call: forcing them through a
building generator is reuse for its own sake). Barns/silos are a small
`agricultural-structure` archetype; fields are a ZONE kind, like the
park and the yard.
- **Candidate good (structures):** "a barn is one honest volume with a
  roof that dominates; a silo is a landmark; both read as the same
  card/kit family as the city."
- **Gate sketch:** as registered (03/07/08/09 exempt) — few rules judge;
  the real quality control is the donor sheet and the zone rules for
  field dressing.
- **Canon coverage:** UNCOVERED entirely (slot 3's flocked ground is the
  nearest neighbour) → the strongest case for a new canon slot before
  this archetype starts.
- **OWNER CALLS:** □ confirm fields-as-zones □ how far from the city do
  farms live (board edges? separate boards?) □ canon slot for the
  agricultural look before geometry.
