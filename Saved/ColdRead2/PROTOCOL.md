## Hardening (design-session review, 2026-08-30, all adopted)

- **CAMERA TRANSFORMS RECORDED BESIDE THE FRAMES** — a JSON sidecar per
  named framing with exact loc/rot. This is what makes read #2
  comparable to read #1 and re-shootable for read #3; without it "same
  framing" is a memory, and the works-brick before/after paid the cost
  of exactly that gap today.
- **VEHICLES STAY IN FRAME — ruled 2026-08-30, design session, after
  the first draft of this clause said the opposite.** The decisive
  argument: "vehicles carrying wall paint" is one of this read's four
  measured subjects — stripping vehicles would UN-MEASURE it and quietly
  return a pass by absence, the same failure shape as a gate going
  quiet. Comparability with read #1 (whose reader saw the vehicles and
  produced the diecast finding) is the bonus, not the reason.
  **Doctrine, stated for the record: the no-vehicles constraint protects
  GATE evidence, whose subject is the architecture; a cold read's
  subject is what a person sees, and a street with no cars is not what a
  person sees. Different instruments, different admissibility.**
  Three conditions attach:
    1. Every read #2 frame lives under Saved/ColdRead2/ and its sidecar
       carries an explicit NOT GATE-ADMISSIBLE marker — the file stops
       the future frame-borrower, not this conversation's memory.
    2. Same DRESSING, not merely "vehicles present": the current
       dressing manifest (DRESSING.json beside the frames) is the
       record; any delta against read #1's condition is LOGGED so the
       reader's response can be attributed rather than guessed.
    3. BLD2 and all bench apparatus (SHELF / STUDY / DONOR-shelf /
       SWATCH instruments) stay OUT of every read framing — instruments
       are not city, and two BLD2 actors may carry today's works-brick,
       which has no look verdict of its own.
- **THE FRAMINGS MUST NOT HIDE THE TELL**: read #1's finding was the
  paper tell, so at least one framing must put a tell-prone surface
  (coping caps, band tops — the surfaces where a woven/canvas hatch
  would show at player zoom) squarely in frame. A read composed away
  from its known risk is not a read. Live data point — one model, one
  pair of eyes, not generalised: freshly baked deco coping caps read as
  a coarse woven hatch at player zoom in judge mode. These meshes carry
  card_heavy Phase 1, so this is what the surface looks like AFTER the
  split, not before. Read #2 should be composed so the reader can see
  it. Named arithmetic suspect (not a conclusion): card_heavy kept
  tooth=0.006 — one paper tile per ~167 uu, under three tiles spanning
  the 463 uu player-zoom frame — while the split stocks derived their
  tilings from what each map depicts. Deliberately NOT fixed pre-read:
  the read is the instrument for whether this is a defect or the
  handmade-card read, and retuning first would destroy attribution to
  the split.
- **THE PLAYER-ZOOM FRAMING HAS A NAMED SUBJECT, pre-registered before
  frames exist**: a deco coping-cap / band-top run at 900 uu on the
  street (exact actor + transform recorded in the sidecar when the
  street is repaired and framed). Choosing the target after seeing
  frames is the composed-away failure this clause exists to prevent.
  **Amendment at certification (2026-08-30): this clause is satisfiable
  IN JUDGE MODE ONLY.** At zoom range the locked show optic (f/2.8 on
  the 400 back) has a focal band thinner than any surface feature — the
  tell surface is soft in show no matter where focus lands (measured,
  not assumed). A reader's silence about surfaces in show mode is NOT a
  surface pass; the surface question is asked of the judge frame, which
  is why judge-first ordering is load-bearing and not stylistic.
